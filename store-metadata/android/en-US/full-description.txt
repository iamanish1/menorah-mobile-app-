Menorah Health helps men find and book one-to-one support with counsellors reviewed through Menorah's verification process.

Use Menorah Health to:

• Browse counsellor profiles, professional information and areas of focus.
• View hourly rates before booking a paid session.
• Search and filter counsellors to find a suitable option.
• Book and manage eligible one-to-one sessions.
• Use secure in-app chat with your assigned counsellor.
• Complete optional wellbeing check-ins and review your own results.
• Read practical mental-health and wellbeing articles.
• Manage your profile, notification preferences and account settings.

Counsellor verification reflects Menorah's review of submitted professional information before account activation. It does not guarantee a particular outcome or replace your own judgement when choosing support.

Wellbeing check-ins and articles are educational and informational. They do not provide a diagnosis, medical advice or emergency support.

Menorah Health is not an emergency or crisis service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency service.

Features, session availability and prices can vary by counsellor and location. Review the counsellor's profile, hourly rate and booking details before confirming a session.
